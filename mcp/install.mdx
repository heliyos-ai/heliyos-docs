Every client runs the same command, `npx -y @heliyos/heliyos-mcp`, with your key in the `HELIYOS_API_KEY` environment variable. Replace `YOUR_HELIYOS_API_KEY` in the snippets with your key.

<Warning>
  Config files that hold the key in plain text are as sensitive as the key. Keep them out of shared folders and repositories. Where a client can read the key from your environment or a secret store, prefer that.
</Warning>

## Check it works

Ask your agent: *"Which Heliyos workspace am I connected to?"* It should call `heliyos_organization_get` and answer with your workspace's name. If it does not, see [Troubleshooting](/mcp/troubleshooting).
